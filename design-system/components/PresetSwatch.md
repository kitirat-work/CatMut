Five one-tap wallpaper backgrounds in a 5-column grid: `mat-green` (default), `mat-charcoal`, `mat-grey`, `mat-plum`, `mat-periwinkle`. Each button is `field` with a `line` border, the color chip uses `radius-sm`, and the Thai name sits under it. The selected one gets an `accent` border plus a 1px inset `accent` ring. Picking a preset also sets line intensity and resets ink to automatic. The consumer provides the name, the hex and the intensity. A free hex field and color picker sit beside the presets for any other color.
