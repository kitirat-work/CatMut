A small drawing of the wallpaper's parts, not a component to build. From the bottom: the grid (minor `mat-line-minor`, major `mat-line-major`), the vertical label in the top-left strip, then the frame (`mat-line-frame`). On top: the quote plate, centred in the frame, and the paragraph plate in the bottom-right corner. Plates are filled with the background color and their edges land on grid lines, inset so no line is cut. All text shares one font.
