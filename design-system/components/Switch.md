For on/off options: shadow, text plate, quote, paragraph. The track is 34 by 20 with `radius-pill`; off is `line`, on is `accent`. The thumb is `field` and moves 14px in 150ms. The label sits to the right in `body` weight 500. Turning a switch off dims and disables the controls it owns (opacity .45). The consumer provides the label and the checked state. Never use a switch for a choice between two named options; use chips.
